# Cezary Baraniecki

I build user-facing products at the intersection of AI, product judgment, and hands-on prototyping.
I like ambiguous workflows where the useful product is not another chatbot, but a clearer decision,
a safer handoff, or a frustrating task made easier.

My projects are working proofs with inspectable behavior, synthetic demos, and honest limits.
They are shaped around human control: the software can recommend, organize, and explain, while the
person keeps authority over consequential actions.

## Current work

### [WatchSignal](https://github.com/czzzry/watchsignal) · [Live showcase](https://watchsignal-web.vercel.app/showcase)

A phone-first movie picker for two people with different tastes.
Each person reacts privately, WatchSignal protects hard no votes, and the result explains the
strongest shared choice instead of hiding behind a score.

### [Product Decision Compiler](https://github.com/czzzry/product-decision-compiler) · [Interactive proof](https://czzzry.github.io/product-decision-compiler/)

A read-only decision layer for AI-assisted delivery.
It connects approved product intent to Linear and GitHub evidence, then brings only meaningful
exceptions back for human review.

### [Threadwise](https://github.com/czzzry/threadwise) · [Interactive demo](https://czzzry.github.io/threadwise/)

A teachable inbox companion that classifies email, explains its decisions beside Gmail, and
previews the broader impact of a correction before anything changes provider-side.

I am also building [Pyrenees Selects](https://github.com/czzzry/pyrenees-selects), a local screening
room for a large drone archive, and [Local Scriptorium](https://github.com/czzzry/local-scriptorium),
an evidence-first retrieval study over Late Antique texts.

**Working principle:** start with the user decision, build the smallest complete workflow, and make
the evidence, uncertainty, and authority boundaries visible.
